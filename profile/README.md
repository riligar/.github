## 🌟 About RiLiGar

**RiLiGar** is a cutting-edge technology company focused on creating **high-performance SDKs** and innovative tools for developers. Our mission is to **democratize development** of intelligent, complex, and scalable systems, making them simple and efficient.

We believe the future of technology lies in **artificial intelligence** and **intelligent automation**. That's why we build the fundamental blocks that enable teams to create extraordinary applications without traditional complexity.

---

## 🏗️ Our Technology Pillars

### 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence & Agents**
We develop robust frameworks for creating AI agents, simplifying integration with LLMs and custom tools. Our approach makes AI accessible to any developer.

### ⚙️ **Unified SDK Ecosystem**
We create a cohesive suite of SDKs (agents, workflows, applications, orchestrator) that dramatically accelerate the development of robust and scalable software.

### 🚀 **Extreme Performance & Scalability**
From SQLite databases with real-time replication to tools written in Go, we always seek the most performant and efficient solutions in the market.

### 📚 **Developer Experience (DevEx)**
We believe exceptional documentation and internal tools are the key to productivity. That's why we build our own solutions, like **Knowledge**.
