# Welcome to RiLiGar 👋

> **Building the blocks for the next generation of intelligent applications.**

We are a technology company focused on creating high-performance SDKs and tools for developers. Our passion is to empower teams to build complex, intelligent, and scalable systems with simplicity and efficiency.

## Our Pillars

### 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence & Agents**
We create frameworks for developing AI agents, simplifying integration with LLMs and custom tools.

### ⚙️ **SDK Ecosystem**
We develop a cohesive suite of SDKs (`agents`, `workflows`, `applications`, `orchestrator`) to accelerate the development of robust software.

### 🚀 **Performance and Scalability**
From SQLite databases with real-time replication to tools written in Go, we always seek the most performant and lean solutions.

### 📚 **Developer Experience (DevEx)**
We believe that great documentation and internal tools are key to productivity. That's why we build our own solutions, like `knowledge`.
